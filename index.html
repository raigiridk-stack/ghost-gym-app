Before you start
Place your phone from the side, 2–3 m (6–10 ft) away. Keep your full body — head through feet — visible. Use bodyweight or a light practice load while testing. Ghost gives movement cues, not a medical or injury-risk assessment.
Privacy: Ghost V7.1 processes camera frames in your browser for pose landmarks. This prototype does not record or upload your workout video.

How Ghost judges this prototype

Ghost watches hip, knee, ankle and shoulder landmarks from the side. A rep begins as you descend, reaches the bottom when knee flexion passes the configured threshold, and counts after you stand back up. Feedback focuses on squat depth and torso consistency. If landmark confidence is poor, Ghost says it cannot assess instead of guessing.
